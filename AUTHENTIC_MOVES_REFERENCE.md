## ⚡ GODS

### **1. POSEIDON** - God of the Sea
**Weapon**: Trident
**Fighting Style**: Extreme speed, afterimage-creating thrusts

**Abilities:**
- **Amphitrite** (Q): Incessant circular trident thrusts at blinding speed creating afterimages
- **Chione Tyro Demeter** (E): Aerial attack - engulfs opponent in a storm of thrusts from above
- **40 Day Flood** (R/Ultimate): Creates enormous dome of afterimages like a whirlpool, combining divine might and godly speed

**Special**: Hydrokinesis (control over divine waters)

---

### **2. ZEUS** - King of the Gods
**Weapon**: Bare fists
**Fighting Style**: Overwhelming speed and power, muscle control

**Abilities:**
- **Meteor Jab** (Q): Powerful striking technique with enhanced speed
- **Axe Kick** (E): Devastating kicking technique
- **Time Transcending Fist (TFTST)** (R/Ultimate): Attack so fast it transcends time (0.00000001 seconds)

**Special**: Adamas Form - Ultimate transformation with indestructible body

---

### **3. THOR** - God of Thunder
**Weapon**: Mjölnir (Divine Hammer)
**Fighting Style**: Thunder Berserker - Lightning-infused hammer strikes

**Abilities:**
- **Thor's Hammer** (Q): Imbues Mjölnir with divine lightning for devastating strike
- **Geirröd** (E): Enhanced hammer technique with increased power
- **Awakened Thunder Hammer** (R/Ultimate): Flings Mjölnir with extreme velocity before slamming down with overwhelming force

**Special**: Electrokinesis, teleportation

---

### **4. SHIVA** - God of Destruction
**Weapon**: Four arms (bare fists)
**Fighting Style**: Greatest brawler, four-armed multidirectional attacks

**Abilities:**
- **Tandava Dance** (Q): Four-armed ferocious volley of blows like cannon fire
- **Devaloka** (E): Enhanced striking technique with all four arms
- **Tandava Karma** (R/Ultimate): Transformation - skin turns red, tattoos black, blue flames cover body, massively amplified power

**Special**: Four arms allow simultaneous attacks from multiple angles

---

### **5. HADES** - God of the Underworld
**Weapon**: Bident
**Fighting Style**: Powerful bident techniques with wind generation

**Abilities:**
- **Persephone-Lore** (Q): Concentrates spiral winds around bident tip
- **Persephone-Kallichore** (E): Strikes ground generating wind blade waves
- **Persephone Titan** (R/Ultimate): Cracks terrain with tremendous power, creates devastating wind blades

**Special**: Wind pressure attacks, afterimage-speed strikes

## 👤 HUMANS

### **6. ADAM** - Father of Humanity
**Weapon**: Knuckledusters
**Fighting Style**: Counter-based, perfect technique replication

**Abilities:**
- **Eyes of the Lord** (Q): Divine Reflection - perfectly copies any move witnessed
- **Divine Counter** (E): Uses opponent's technique against them instantly
- **Time Punch Rush** (R/Ultimate): Rapid barrage of time-transcending punches (copied from Zeus)

**Special**: Can copy ANY technique he sees, fights even after death through willpower

---

### **7. QIN SHI HUANG** - First Emperor
**Weapon**: Sword
**Fighting Style**: Chi redirection, defensive counters

**Abilities:**
- **Mount Tai Dragon Claw** (Q): Piercing jab attack
- **Tortoise Ripple/Heavenly Hand** (E): Defensive chi redirection technique using air pockets
- **Chi You - Star Burst** (R/Ultimate): Combined technique with Valkyrie power

**Special**: Can see and manipulate chi/air pockets to redirect enemy attacks

---

### **8. JACK THE RIPPER** - The Ripper
**Weapon**: Knives, various improvised weapons
**Fighting Style**: Assassin tactics, environmental traps

**Abilities:**
- **Knife Barrage** (Q): Rapid throwing knife attacks
- **Guidance of the Nocturne** (E): Arm extension technique for increased reach and momentum
- **Dear God** (R/Ultimate): Lethal assassination technique with divine weapon

**Special**: Soul Eye - sees emotions as colors, organ manipulation to avoid fatal damage

---

### **9. LÜ BU** - Flying General
**Weapon**: Halberd (Sky Piercer)
**Fighting Style**: Overwhelming offensive power, relentless strikes

**Abilities:**
- **Halberd Barrage** (Q): Consecutive halberd swings (can do 36,000+ non-stop)
- **Flying General Strike** (E): Powerful aerial halberd slash
- **Sky Eater** (R/Ultimate): Strongest attack - swings halberd with force to part clouds for miles

**Special**: Unmatched weapon mastery, fights through catastrophic injuries

---

### **10. BUDDHA** - The Enlightened One
**Weapon**: Six Realms Staff
**Fighting Style**: Precognitive combat, versatile transformations

**Abilities:**
- **Future Vision Strike** (Q): Preemptive dodge and counter using precognition
- **Shield of Ahimsa** (E): Defensive staff technique, pushes back attacks
- **Mahāpāramitā / Nirvana** (R/Ultimate): Ultimate technique that cuts through anything

**Special**: Future Vision - sees opponent's soul and predicts all movements, Staff transforms to adapt to situations

- R Ultimates: 90-120 damage (high cooldown 18-25s)

2. **Zeus**: Speed-based damage multiplier, transformation

5. **Hades**: Wind blade projectiles, ground slam AOE
6. **Adam**: Copy last ability used against him
7. **Qin**: Damage reflection/reduction shield

9. **Lü Bu**: Charge-up mechanic for Sky Eater
